Hi! I'm Miguel. I'm a postdoc researcher at the section for Visual Computing at DTU Compute (Denmark) with [Rasmus Larsen](https://www.dtu.dk/english/person/rasmus-larsen?id=1431&entity=profile). My current research focuses on deep learning, anomaly detection, ships ([AIS data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automatic_identification_system)), and satellite imagery.

Previously, I worked with [Anders B. Dahl](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/persons/anders-bjorholm-dahl) on topology-focused image segmentation, with my work being published at BMVC (Oral) and CVPR. My Ph.D., with [Jussi Tohka](https://www.jussitohka.net/) as my supervisor, focused on rodent brain MRI segmentation.

My research interests are: deep learning, computer vision, image segmentation, topology, anomaly detection, and efficient DL. I have extensive experience working with medical images/volumes, including MRI, CT, X-ray (clinical and synchrotron), and electron-microscopy. More recently, I have been shifting my focus to satellite imagery and marine/maritime-related data, including AIS and underwater images.

I'm open for collaborations and other academic activities. I have organized several workshops and given guest lectures. Feel free to contact me: jmvma (at) dtu.dk.
